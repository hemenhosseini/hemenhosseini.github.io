Physicist working on ultrafast spectroscopy, using femtosecond light pulses, from the visible to the extreme ultraviolet, to watch how electrons, excitons and spins move in quantum materials.

At Elettra Sincrotrone Trieste I work with the FERMI seeded free-electron laser and the CITIUS high-harmonic source on XUV pump–probe experiments. My background spans semiconductors, transition-metal dichalcogenides, halide perovskites and van der Waals magnets, studied with transient absorption, time-resolved MOKE and ultrafast holographic microscopy. I completed my PhD at Politecnico di Milano, a postdoc at CNR-IFN in Milan, and the Master in High Performance Computing at SISSA, so I also write a fair amount of scientific and instrument-control software.
