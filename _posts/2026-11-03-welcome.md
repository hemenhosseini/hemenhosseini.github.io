Welcome to my website. I'm a physicist at Elettra Sincrotrone Trieste, where I use femtosecond
laser and free-electron-laser pulses to study fast processes in quantum materials.

I plan to use this space for:

- **Explainers** on ultrafast and XUV spectroscopy techniques,
- **Notes from the lab** on instrumentation, lasers and data analysis,
- **Computing**: scientific Python, HPC and instrument-control software.

Equations work too, written in LaTeX. For example, the pump–probe signal is the differential
transmission

$$
\frac{\Delta T}{T}(\tau) = \frac{T_{\text{pump on}}(\tau) - T_{\text{pump off}}}{T_{\text{pump off}}}.
$$
